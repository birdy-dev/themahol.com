Join us every Tuesday for an energetic, creative, and growth-oriented dance experience. Our guest teachers bring more than a decade of experience teaching across Canada, exploring styles such as Hip Hop and Animation to help you grow, groove, and find your own style.

Sessions focus on foundations, musicality, drills, technique, and the joy of moving freely. It’s a place to build confidence and discipline alongside a community that feels like family.

All levels. All ages. All backgrounds.

This is where rhythm, culture, and community come together. Let’s build the mahol (vibe) together.
